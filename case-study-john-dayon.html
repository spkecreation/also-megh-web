PRODUCT CASE STUDY 01 / PREVIEW

John Dayon
Behind the product.

A product story about turning a broad creative portfolio into a clear, navigable experience.

The decisions
are taking shape.

This preview frames the product problem, the core experience decisions, and the intended user outcome. Detailed research evidence and verified metrics will be added when available.

  1. Proposed role

    Product design / UX / prototyping

  2. Problem

    Visitors need to understand the offer quickly and move from introduction to relevant work without losing the creator’s personality.

  3. Approach

    Prioritize the main journey, simplify the information hierarchy, and prototype a portfolio system that makes exploration feel deliberate.

  4. Intended outcome

    A clearer creator product with an immediate value proposition and direct paths into selected work.
